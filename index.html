Writeflo

Write without looking back. Read it tomorrow.

Writeflo is a minimal, distraction-free writing app. Your words gently fade as you type, so you can't stop to edit — you just flow. What you write locks until the next day, when you can finally read it back with fresh eyes. Everything stays private and on your device.

Common questions

Why can't I read what I just wrote?
That's by design. Each piece unlocks the next day so you return to it with fresh perspective.

Where is my writing stored?
Entirely on your device. Writeflo has no accounts and no servers — your words never leave your phone.

Can I export my writing?
Yes. Once a piece unlocks, you can read, export, and share it using the standard iOS share sheet.
