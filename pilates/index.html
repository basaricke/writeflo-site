Pilates Planner

Build and teach your classes.

Pilates Planner is a planning and teaching tool for instructors who build their own classes. Start from a library of 138 movements, arrange them into a flow, and see which transitions would break the class's rhythm and what the flow is actually working. Then teach it with a runner that keeps time and counts reps with a tap, from your iPhone or your Apple Watch. Everything stays on your device.

Common questions

Can I add my own movements?
Yes. Your own movements sit alongside the built-in library of 138 and can be searched, filtered, and added to any flow.

What are the transition hints?
As you build a flow, the app flags moves that would force the class to get up and reset, so you can smooth the sequence before you teach it.

How does the Apple Watch app work?
The watch companion runs the flow you've prepared on your iPhone, so you can follow the sequence from your wrist instead of holding a phone in front of a class.

Where are my flows stored?
Entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no subscription and no servers. If you delete the app, your flows and session history are deleted with it.
