Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Pilates Planner ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

Information we collect

None. Pilates Planner does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. The app has no user accounts, requires no sign-up, and has no network features.

Your data stays on your device

Your movements, flows, session history and notes are stored locally on your iPhone. They are never uploaded to a cloud service or shared with us or any third party. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

Apple Watch

To run a flow from your wrist, the iPhone app passes the flow you've prepared to the companion Apple Watch app on your own devices. This data does not pass through any server of ours.

No analytics or tracking

Pilates Planner does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or tracking software. It contains no third-party SDKs, and it does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Pilates Planner does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information from children is gathered or stored.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at basaricke@gmail.com.
