Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 5, 2026

Writeflo ("the app") is designed to be private by default. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with your information.

Information we collect

None. Writeflo does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. The app has no user accounts, requires no sign-up, and does not ask for your name, email, or any other identifying details to function.

Your writing stays on your device

Everything you write in Writeflo is stored locally on your device only. Your entries are never uploaded, synced to a cloud service, or shared with us or any third party. If you delete the app, your local data is removed with it.

No analytics or tracking

Writeflo does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We do not use third-party SDKs that collect data, and we do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

Sharing and exporting

When you choose to export or share a piece of writing, that action uses Apple's standard iOS share sheet and is entirely under your control. Writeflo only shares content when you explicitly ask it to.

Children's privacy

Writeflo does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no data at all, no personal information from children is gathered or stored.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version introduces new features — this policy will be updated and the "last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact us at basaricke@gmail.com.
